# 42 Porto Piscine – Summer 2025

This repository is a record of my experience during the 42 Porto Piscine — a challenging and immersive 26-day bootcamp focused on programming in C. Over this period, I explored the fundamental structure of C programs and functions, along with developing skills in logic and teamwork. 

It pushed me to learn autonomously and work closely with fellow participants, many of whom were starting from scratch. It was my first real experience coding in C, and I progressed from basic syntax to manipulating strings and arrays, handling arguments in main, and implementing dynamic memory allocation.

---

The experience was extremely challenging, yet it was balanced by the incredible people I met along the way. I shared this journey with an amazing group, and the constant exchange of ideas and mutual support made the process both rewarding and fun — and I am really proud of my performance throughout this journey.
